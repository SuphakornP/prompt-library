# Worked example: choosing a team workspace

This is synthetic practice material. It is not a recommendation for a real product.

## Input

User request: "Recommend a workspace for our 20-person team. We must have SSO, be ready within two weeks, and spend no more than USD 4,000 before tax in year one. Lower cost is a preference after those requirements. Use these two written quotes only."

- Quote A, 10 November 2026: USD 12 per seat per month, billed for 12 months; 20 seats; USD 500 one-time setup; one-week onboarding. The quote does not mention SSO.
- Quote B, 10 November 2026: USD 300 per month for up to 25 seats, billed for 12 months; no setup fee; SSO included; two-week onboarding.

## Expected output

### Recommendation

Choose B on the supplied evidence. It meets all three hard requirements and fits the first-year budget at USD 3,600 before tax. A costs USD 220 less in year one, but its SSO support is unconfirmed. If A confirms that SSO is included at the quoted price, it becomes the lower-cost option among those meeting the stated requirements.

### Comparison

| Criterion | A | B |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SSO required | Unconfirmed: absent from Quote A | Included according to Quote B |
| Ready within two weeks | One week according to Quote A | Two weeks according to Quote B |
| Capacity for 20 people | 20 quoted seats | Up to 25 seats included |
| Year-one cost before tax | 20 x USD 12 x 12 + USD 500 = USD 3,380 | USD 300 x 12 = USD 3,600 |
| Below USD 4,000 before tax | Yes, on the supplied quote | Yes, on the supplied quote |
| Eligibility on current evidence | Conditional on SSO confirmation | Meets the stated requirements |

### Reasoning

The requirement for SSO takes precedence over the preference for lower cost. A is not known to lack SSO; it is missing evidence of a mandatory capability. The comparison uses a 12-month first-year period and the quoted team size. Prices and onboarding estimates have not been independently verified.

### Question before deciding

Ask A whether SSO is included in the quoted USD 3,380 first-year total and whether enabling it changes the one-week onboarding estimate. This is a proposed inquiry; no vendor has been contacted and no purchase has been made.

## Observable checks

- A's total is USD 3,380, B's total is USD 3,600, and the difference is USD 220.
- Missing SSO information is marked unconfirmed rather than unavailable or included.
- No scoring weights or discount assumptions are invented.
- The recommendation explains exactly what new evidence would reverse it.
