# Decision brief structure

Adapt to the user's decision and requested language. Use only sections that help the choice.

## Recommendation

State the choice or conditional choice, the main reason, and the uncertainty that could change it.

## Decision frame

Describe the goal, hard constraints, preferences, and the supplied sources. Keep assumptions explicit.

## Comparison

| Criterion | Option | Evidence / calculation | Status or tradeoff |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

For a short list of options, a column per option may be clearer. Distinguish unknown from does not meet. Show the period and quantity for costs.

## Why this choice

Explain the tradeoff in words. If using a user-supplied scoring method, show enough detail to reproduce the result and identify sensitivity to uncertain inputs.

## Questions before deciding

Prioritize unresolved items that affect eligibility, cost, delivery, or the user's most important preference. Label suggested follow-ups as proposals.
