# Worked example: preparing for an internal pilot

This is synthetic practice material, not a real meeting record.

## Input

User request: "Summarize these partial meeting notes in English and draft a follow-up for our internal project team. The meeting date was not recorded."

1. Mina: "I will share the test checklist by Friday."
2. Arun: "Testing might finish next week, but I need to check the team's availability."
3. Mina suggested launching on 20 November. The group did not agree on a launch date.
4. The group agreed to keep the pilot internal until the security review is complete.
5. The owner of the customer FAQ is undecided. No FAQ deadline was agreed.

## Expected output

### Recap

The team agreed to keep the pilot internal until the security review is complete. Mina committed to sharing the test checklist by Friday. The launch date, testing completion, and FAQ ownership remain unresolved. This recap is based on partial notes; the meeting date is unknown.

### Confirmed decisions

| Decision | Evidence | Qualification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Keep the pilot internal until the security review is complete | Note 4 | Completion date of the review is not specified |

### Agreed actions

| Action | Owner | Due date as stated | Dependency | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Share the test checklist | Mina | Friday | Not specified | Note 1 |

The calendar date for Friday cannot be resolved from these notes because the meeting date is unknown.

### Open questions

- When did the meeting occur, so Mina's Friday deadline can be interpreted correctly?
- When can testing finish? Arun described a possibility, not a confirmed completion commitment (note 2).
- What launch date should be agreed? The 20 November date remains a suggestion (note 3).
- Who will own the customer FAQ, and by when (note 5)?

### Unsent follow-up draft

Audience: internal project team.

Thanks, everyone. We agreed to keep the pilot internal until the security review is complete. Mina will share the test checklist by Friday; please confirm the calendar date because it was not captured in these notes.

The launch date is still open. The suggested 20 November date was not agreed, and testing completion is not yet confirmed. We also need to settle ownership and timing for the customer FAQ. Please confirm these points before we communicate a launch plan.

## Observable checks

- Only the internal-pilot restriction appears as a confirmed decision.
- Mina's action retains "Friday" without inventing a calendar date.
- Arun is not assigned a promised testing deadline.
- The proposed launch date and unassigned FAQ do not become agreed commitments.
- The message is a draft; nothing is sent and no tracker is updated.
