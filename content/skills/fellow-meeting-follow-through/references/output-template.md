# Meeting follow-through output template

Use the sections relevant to the user's request. Translate headings into the user's requested language. The labels below describe fields, not content to invent.

## Recap

Meeting purpose, outcome, and the most important next steps. Note whether the source is a full transcript or partial notes.

## Confirmed decisions

| Decision | Evidence | Qualification |
| --- | --- | --- |

If no decision is confirmed, say so. Put proposals and unresolved choices in the open-questions section.

## Agreed actions

| Action / deliverable | Owner | Due date as stated | Dependency | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Use "Not specified" for unknown owners, deadlines, or dependencies. Add a resolved calendar date only when the source context supports it.

## Open questions and suggested next steps

List unresolved conflicts and the few questions that would most improve the plan. Label any assistant-suggested action as a suggestion, with no implied commitment from a meeting participant.

## Unsent follow-up draft

State the intended audience, or the internal-audience assumption. Draft a short recap with confirmed actions and clear requests to resolve outstanding questions. Do not imply it has been sent.
