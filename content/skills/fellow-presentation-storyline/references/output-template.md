# Presentation storyboard structure

Adapt the number and order of slides to the user. Translate headings and content into the requested language.

## Presentation frame

- Audience and purpose
- Desired decision or understanding
- Main message
- Time or slide constraints, including any working assumptions

## Slide sequence

For each slide, include the relevant fields:

- **Message title:** A clear, evidence-supported statement.
- **On-slide content:** The few facts or points the audience needs.
- **Visual direction:** A proposed layout, chart, diagram, or image concept supported by available material.
- **Evidence:** Source location or an explicitly missing fact.
- **Speaker notes:** Explanation, transition, and any qualification that should not be lost.

## Closing ask

State what the audience is being asked to decide or do. Distinguish a proposed plan from an approved commitment.

## Production questions

List missing facts, assets, or constraints that could change the story. This is a storyboard, not a generated or visually verified slide deck.
