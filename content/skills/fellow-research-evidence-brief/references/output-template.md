# Evidence brief structure

Adapt to the user's question and audience. Use the requested language. Omit empty sections where they do not help, while making an absence of evidence visible.

## Answer and scope

State the question, the best supported answer, and the material reviewed. Say whether the scope is excerpts, full supplied documents, or partial notes.

## Findings and evidence

| Finding | Source and locator | What was observed | Material limitation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

Keep evidence distinct from interpretation. Preserve denominators, measurement periods, and stated collection methods. Explicitly mark an unknown method; do not transfer one source's method to another source.

## Synthesis and disagreements

Explain what the sources jointly suggest, where they disagree, and whether the differences prevent a common conclusion. Label your own inferences.

## Implications and next questions

List the practical implication supported now and the information that could change it. Clearly identify proposed research or actions.

## Source register

| Source ID | Supplied title or description | Supplied date and location | Coverage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

Use only metadata present in the input. Describe missing fields rather than inventing them.
