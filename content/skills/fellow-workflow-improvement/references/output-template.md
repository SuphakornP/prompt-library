# Workflow improvement structure

Adapt to the size of the process and the user's requested language. Keep current facts separate from proposed changes.

## Main recommendation

State the observed issue, the proposed small change, and what the experiment will reveal.

## Current process

Describe the trigger and completion condition.

| Step | Current role | Input and action | Output / handoff | Evidence or unknown |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Number only the known sequence. Put an activity whose timing is unknown in a separate "Unplaced activity" row or section, with the missing sequencing information stated.

## Pain points and constraints

| Observation | Supporting example or measure | Possible cause | Constraint to preserve |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

Label a cause as a hypothesis unless the input establishes it. Keep overlapping measures separate.

## Proposed process change

Show only the affected steps and how exceptions are handled. Identify retained approvals and any new responsibility requiring agreement.

## Small experiment

- Proposed scope and review point
- Baseline available, or how to establish it
- Measures of benefit and possible additional burden
- Conditions for keeping, adjusting, or stopping the change

## Open questions

List only missing details that could change the diagnosis or prevent the experiment. Suggested actions and owners remain proposals until agreed.
